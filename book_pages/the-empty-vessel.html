A COSMIC NOIR NOVEL

The Empty Vessel

Geometry is the new god. Emptiness is the only shield.


In the chilled halls of the Hokkaido RMF facility, Eve Hendrickson is more than a daughter—she is a coordinate. Marked as the perfect bridge to the Sub-Cuneus, her soul is a house waiting for a tenant. When the Bethesda Procedure pierces the lock of reality, Eve is cast into the Mindscape—a monochrome cathedral of shadow where memories are fuel and the "Dragon" waits to inhabit the porous.

Read if you love high-concept sci-fi, mind-bending physics, and the chilling realization that being "empty" isn't a curse—it’s a weapon.
