A GOTHIC MEDICAL THRILLER

Graveyard Shift at St. Jude’s

Dr. Harper Evans traded her Manhattan penthouse for a subterranean bunker in Chicago after a tragic surgical "incident."[cite: 1]


Now, she operates at St. Jude’s, a sanctuary where the patients are gargoyles, mer-folk, and banshees[cite: 1]. But a new horror has arrived: the "Mundane Strain." This aggressive virus doesn't kill—it erases. It attacks the "otherness" of supernatural beings, rewriting their DNA into common human biology[cite: 1]. From high-tech incubators to the ancient sewers of Chicago, Harper must race to synthesize a cure before the city's magical soul is harvested for profit[cite: 1].

"Physics is just a variable we haven't quantified yet. I don't trust fairy dust, but I trust the structural integrity of calcium and mineral deposits." — Dr. Harper Evans[cite: 1]
