A COSMIC NOIR THRILLER

The Keyhole Spectacle

Los Angeles, 1950. Elias Thorne sits in arectangular box that smells of stale Lucky Strikes, waiting for "The Twitch"—a localized throb behind his eye that signals a lie[cite: 2].


Hired to find the vanished starlet Lola Vane, Elias is pulled into the "Keyhole Club," a transit station for Tourists from a perfect, mathematical dimension who pay to inhabit human "vessels" just to feel the sting of entropy[cite: 2]. As the Bureau prepares a national broadcast to "Standardize" the country, Elias must use his own trauma as the "Grit" to jam the signal and save the city’s messy, human soul[cite: 2].

"The mud is what makes us human. You can't just delete the parts of the story you don't like." — Elias Thorne[cite: 2]
